DARKSOL / THE ESSENTIALS

Introduction

DarkSol helps you send and receive privately by comparing privacy pools and routing transfers through supported protocols.

Choose a route using real pool activity, estimated privacy, and cost. Your wallet approves the transactions; the selected protocol handles the shielded funds.

Private send

Send from your wallet through a privacy pool to a recipient. Choose the asset, amount, and destination, compare available routes, then approve the transfer. The pool helps separate the deposit from the withdrawal.

Private receive

Create a payment link without sharing your receiving wallet. The payer deposits into a privacy pool; you collect the funds later to a chosen address. You'll find this feature under Payment links in the app.

Red team

The red team tests privacy from an outside observer's perspective. It examines public deposits and withdrawals for possible links using amounts, timing, address reuse, and pool history.

Its findings show the evidence and its limits. A suspected link is an inference, not proof of ownership. Finding no link does not prove anonymity.

Core concepts

Privacy pool
A protocol that holds shielded funds and verifies private transfers.
Shielding
Moving public funds into a pool. Withdrawing makes the destination and amount public again.
Private note
The secret data used to spend shielded funds. Keep recovery material private.
Privacy estimate
A score based on pool activity and your transfer. Amount, timing, and available cover matter; the score is not a guarantee.

Architecture

DarkSol connects three parts:

  1. Browser & walletChoose routes, prepare private proofs, and approve transactions.
  2. Privacy protocolsHold shielded funds and verify deposits and withdrawals on-chain.
  3. Data & planningIndexers collect public activity. Stored data powers pool comparisons, privacy estimates, and red-team analysis.

The payment-link service stores invoices and payment status. Payment-note spending keys are derived in your browser from your wallet signature.
